AI Visibility

Plan and mockup for review, 2026-11-14. Nothing is built yet.

The plan

What it does

Once a week, for every paid agent, SparkReels asks AI search the questions buyers and sellers in their town ask, then checks each answer for the agent.

  • Named: the agent's name or brokerage is in the answer.
  • Cited: one of the agent's links is listed as a source: website, Halo site, YouTube channel or one of their YouTube videos, Video Business Card page.
  • Cited instead: the sites AI used when it didn't use the agent. Shows what to make next.

The questions: the town's top searches

Once a month, for each town, SparkReels types starters into Google's own search suggestions ("is blue bell pa", "best realtor blue bell pa", "homes for sale blue bell pa", "blue bell pa schools"…) and keeps the phrases that come up most. The top 10 are rewritten as questions someone would ask ChatGPT. Free: no new service. The agent can swap any question for their own.

Towns

Creator 1 town, Producer 2, Influencer 3. The first town is the agent's market from Settings; they add the others. Each town has its own questions and score.

Which AI search

  • ChatGPT search and Perplexity: both can be asked by the app and return their source links.
  • Google AI Overviews: later. It needs a paid search-data service (about $1–2 per agent a month more).

Where it shows

  1. Spark Insights: a new AI Visibility section for the agent (screen 1 below).
  2. Admin → AI Visibility for you: every agent's score and change from last week (screen 2).
  3. Your Monday watch email: one line per agent who moved up or down.

How it's built

  1. Two tables: each agent's questions and links, and each week's results (question, which AI, named yes/no, which of their links were cited, the top sources). Server only, with the new-table grants.
  2. A weekly job (Sunday night, so Monday's email has it): 10 questions × 2 AI searches per agent, 2 at a time so it doesn't hit rate limits.
  3. Matching: name and brokerage in the answer text; links matched by domain, YouTube channel and each video they've published from SparkReels.
  4. The agent screen, then the Admin table, then the email line.
  5. Make a video on a question opens Create with that question as the topic.

Cost

Google's suggestions are free. The weekly checks are 80 AI searches per town a month on the OpenAI and Perplexity accounts you already have: about $1–1.50 per town a month.

To know

  • AI answers change every time and differ by person, so the screen shows a trend, not a promise.
  • New agents will start near zero. That's the reason to keep posting.
  • The first check runs when an agent opens the page the first time, so nobody waits a week to see anything.

Settled

  • The screen names ChatGPT and Perplexity: they are what's being measured.
  • Paid plans only, like the rest of Spark Insights.
  • Questions come from each town's top searches; the agent can swap any.
  • 1 / 2 / 3 towns on Creator / Producer / Influencer.
