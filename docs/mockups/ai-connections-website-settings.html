Claude

Open Claude
  1. Open Customize, then Connectors
  2. Click + Add, then Add custom connector
  3. Name it SparkReels, paste your link, click Continue
  4. Choose Sign in now and Register automatically, then Add
  5. Click Allow on the SparkReels page

Works on every Claude plan.

ChatGPT

Open ChatGPT
  1. Open Plugins on a computer
  2. Click +, then Add custom MCP server
  3. Name it SparkReels and paste your link
  4. Choose OAuth, check I understand and want to continue, click Create as a plugin
  5. Click Allow on the SparkReels page

Works on ChatGPT Plus. On Business or Enterprise, an admin may need to add it.
